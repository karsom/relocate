Moving your family from the UK to Dubai: the 9-month timeline

The order matters more than the effort. Schools, visas, the house and the container all depend on each other. Here's what to do and when, working back from arrival day.

The three deadlines that catch families out
  1. School assessments run 6–9 months ahead. The best Dubai schools fill their places early, and assessments happen before you've seen a house.
  2. UK documents must be legalised before you fly. Your marriage certificate, children's birth certificates and degrees need FCDO legalisation and UAE attestation. Without them, dependant visas stall.
  3. Your pet's clock starts first. Vaccinations, blood tests and the import permit can take months. Some breeds can't enter the UAE at all.
9–6months before

Decide the shape of the move

  • TaxSee a UK tax adviser about your leaving date, residence status and anything you keep in the UK. This drives every other date.
  • SchoolsShortlist 4–6 schools per child in a sit-down meeting with you and your children: curriculum, inspection rating, the school run, and what each child actually wants. Then apply and book assessments.
  • VisasChoose your route: Golden Visa, employment, investor or property. Start legalising UK documents.
  • PetsCheck your pet's breed is allowed. Start the microchip, vaccine and blood-test sequence.
  • UK homeDecide: sell, let or keep. Instruct agents now if selling.

6–3months before

Lock in school and home

  • SchoolsChildren sit their assessments. Accept offers and pay seat deposits.

  • BuyingAgree terms and sign the sale agreement. A purchase of AED 2m+ can support a Golden Visa application.
  • FreightGet three surveys from accredited international movers. Book your date.
  • UK exitGive your UK school notice. Check the term's notice period, which is often a full term.

3–1months before

Paperwork and logistics

  • VisasSubmit the principal's application. Entry permit issued.
  • HomeFor a rental, sign the tenancy and register it (Ejari). Rent is often paid in 1–4 cheques up front. For a purchase, the title transfers at the Land Department.
  • HealthHealth insurance is mandatory in Dubai. Bind family cover to start on arrival.
  • FreightPrepare an inventory and valuation, and arrange transit insurance. Book specialist shippers for art and wine.
  • Home setupBrief furniture and interiors, and book utilities (DEWA) and internet.

4–0weeks before

Pack and close down the UK

  • PetsImport permit issued. Book the pet's flight.
  • ArrivalBook a serviced apartment if the house isn't ready.
