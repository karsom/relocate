# Client Intake Form (Tally spec)

Each field name maps to Airtable (`Families` / `Members` / `Preferences`). Fields marked **[filter]** drive which tasks get generated from `data/task-templates.csv`.

## 1. Contact
- Lead parent name, email, phone (WhatsApp)
- Second parent name, email
- PA name, email: the PA gets a restricted portal login
- Preferred contact channel: Email / WhatsApp / Call / Via PA
- Referred by (partner or person)

## 2. The move
- Target arrival date **[move_date]**
- Move type: Full exit / Split UK–Dubai / Trial year **[move_type]**
- Package: Landing / Family **[package]**
- Founder add-on needed? Yes/No **[founder]**
- Hard deadlines (school term start, sale completion, job start)

## 3. Household
- Children: name, date of birth, current school, current year group, curriculum (British / IB / American / other), special educational needs (Y/N) **[has_kids]**
- Household staff relocating (nanny, driver, housekeeper): count and nationality **[has_staff]**
- Pets: species, breed, count. Note: some breeds are banned in the UAE **[has_pets]**
- Vehicles: ship from the UK, or buy in Dubai **[ship_car]**

## 4. Home in Dubai
- Rent or buy **[buying]**
- Budget: rent per year (AED) or purchase price (AED)
- Preferred areas: Emirates Hills, Dubai Hills, Palm Jumeirah, Jumeirah Golf Estates, Arabian Ranches, Al Barari, Jumeirah, Other
- Bedrooms; must-haves (pool, maid's room, garden, sea view, gated community)
- Maximum school commute (minutes)
- Ongoing property management wanted? **[property_care]**

## 5. Home in the UK
- Keeping, letting, or selling the UK home **[uk_home: keep/let/sell]**
- Other UK property

## 6. Belongings
- Estimated volume: suitcases only / 20ft / 40ft / 2+ containers **[freight]**
- High-value items: art, wine, jewellery, instruments **[high_value]**
- Storage needed in the UK? **[uk_storage]**

## 7. Money & admin (Parent-only section)
- Has a UK tax adviser? Y/N (if No, refer to a partner)
- Banking preferences (private bank, existing international bank)
- Interested in investment introductions? Y/N **[investments]**: introduction to licensed firms only
- Health insurance: family, staff

## 8. Lifestyle
- Faith or community needs, diet, sports and clubs, kids' activities, security level, privacy needs
- "What would make this move a 10/10 for you?" (free text; the core of personalisation)

## Consent
- Privacy notice (UK GDPR + UAE PDPL)
- Acknowledgement that we coordinate and introduce, and do not give tax, legal, immigration or investment advice
