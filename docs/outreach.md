>
> I'm choosing one or two prime brokerages to bring our buyers and tenants to. Before we start, could you confirm:
> 1. Your referral split on sales and rentals for introduced clients.
> 2. What you need from me to pay it. I can invoice from my company, and I'm UAE-resident with a local bank account.
> 3. That it's set out in a written referral agreement, signed before the first introduction.
>
> Could we do 20 minutes this week? I'm in Dubai until [date].

**Rules:** bank transfer only, against an invoice. Get the agreement signed before you send a single client's name.

## 7. Wealth managers (Dubai / DIFC side): outbound introductions

> Hi [Name], I run Relocate, a private relocation service for UK families worth £5–15m moving to Dubai. Once they've landed, most of them need a Dubai-based wealth manager, and I introduce them to one or two firms I trust.
>
> Before I introduce anyone I need to understand:
> 1. Your regulatory status (DFSA / SCA) and minimum client size.
> 2. Whether you have a formal introducer agreement, and what your regulator allows. Any fee will be disclosed to the client in full.
> 3. How you'd look after a family arriving with UK assets and UK tax history.
>
> Coffee in DIFC this week or next?

**Rules:** you introduce, you never advise. Don't comment on products or returns, even casually. The written introducer agreement must come from the firm.

## 8. Staffing agencies (Dubai): nannies, housekeepers, drivers, chefs

> Hi [Name], I'm Karan, founder of Relocate. We move UK families worth £5–15m to Dubai, and nearly every one needs household staff on arrival: usually a nanny, often a housekeeper and a driver, sometimes a chef.
>
> I'm choosing one or two agencies to introduce our families to. Could you tell me:
> 1. Your MOHRE / Tadbeer licensing, and how you vet candidates (references, background checks, trial days).
> 2. Your placement fees and replacement guarantee.
> 3. Whether you work with a referral agreement for introduced families.
>
> Our families are demanding but loyal. Get the first placement right and you'll get their friends. Could we meet this week?

**What to check before signing:** licence valid and checked on MOHRE; replacement guarantee of at least 3 months; staff visa process handled; how candidates are treated (no passport retention, fair contracts). A scandal at an agency becomes your scandal.
