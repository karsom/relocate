# Compliance Guardrails

**Not legal advice.** Confirm with a UK solicitor, a UK tax adviser and a UAE lawyer before taking a first fee.

## Core rule: coordinate and introduce, never advise
| Area | Regulator | What you may do | What you must NOT do |
|---|---|---|---|
| Investments | FCA (UK); DFSA (DIFC), SCA (onshore), VARA (crypto) | Introduce clients to licensed firms under a written introducer agreement | Recommend, promote or "share opportunities"; handle money; earn undisclosed fees |
| Property sales and rentals | RERA / DLD | Refer clients to a RERA-licensed broker; take a referral split *if the broker confirms it can lawfully pay it* | Negotiate as an agent, advertise listings, register deals |
| Property management | RERA | Refer to a licensed property manager; coordinate | Collect rent or hold deposits |
| Visas and Golden Visa | GDRFA / ICP | Coordinate a licensed PRO; manage the checklist | Promise outcomes or timings |
| UK tax exit (Statutory Residence Test, non-dom rules, inheritance tax) | HMRC (advice given by qualified advisers) | Refer to a UK private client tax firm | Comment on day counts or tax positions |
| Freight | — | Refer to and coordinate licensed movers | Hold customs liability |

## Entity
- Now: **UK Ltd**.
- Later: a UAE free-zone company (IFZA/DMCC/Meydan, activity: management consultancy or relocation services) once Dubai substance is real, staff are hired, or partners require it to pay referral fees.
- While the founder is UK resident: a UAE company run from the UK can be UK tax resident (central management and control test), and UK controlled-foreign-company rules may apply. Hold board decisions in Dubai, keep a log of them, and get adviser sign-off.

## Contracts (before client #1)
- Client engagement letter: scope, fee, the no-advice clause, disclosure of partner commissions, liability cap, and a statement that partners are independent.
- Partner agreement: licence confirmation, referral fee terms, data handling, and partner indemnity.
- Introducer agreement for investment firms, drafted by the firm.

## Insurance
Professional indemnity insurance: £1m minimum, rising to £2m+ once there's property or investment exposure.

## Data
- UK GDPR + UAE PDPL privacy notice. Register with the ICO (UK, about £52 a year).
- Passports, Emirates IDs, bank statements and source-of-wealth documents go in an encrypted vault (Tresorit or Proton Drive) only. Airtable stores links, never the files.
- 2FA on every tool. A separate login for each PA. Offboard each family's access 90 days after they land.

## Commissions
Disclose them in writing. Never steer a client to a partner because it pays more; document why each partner was chosen.
