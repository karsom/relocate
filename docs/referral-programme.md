# Referral Programme

How Relocate turns the founder's network into revenue, without wrecking trust or breaking rules.

## Two directions
| Direction | Who | What they get | What you get |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Inbound** (they send you families) | UK wealth managers, private banks, tax advisers, UK prep schools, UK estate agents | Reciprocal introductions, a client who lands well (and stays their client), or a flat thank-you fee where lawful | New families |
| **Outbound** (you send families to them) | Dubai brokers, movers, insurance, staffing, interiors, banks, wealth managers | Qualified, wealthy clients | A disclosed referral fee or split |

The best partners do both. A wealth manager who sends you a family should get that family back for Dubai wealth advice.

## The five-step process (every partner, no exceptions)
1. **Vet.** Check their licence (number and regulator), get 2 references, and see their work or use them yourself once.
2. **Sign.** A written referral agreement before the first introduction. It covers: the fee or split, when it's paid (on completion or on the client's payment), payment by bank transfer against an invoice, how client data is handled, no poaching, and that the partner indemnifies you for their own work.
3. **Introduce.** Use the email template below: a three-way intro with the family's consent, and the client sees that you may receive a fee.
4. **Log.** Add a record to the Airtable `Referrals` table: family, partner, deal type, expected value, commission %, and status Expected.
5. **Collect.** Invoice on completion. The Make.com automation reminds you after 14 days if it's unpaid.

## Fee guide (negotiate up; these are starting points)
| Category | Typical |
|---|---|
| Property sale | 25–50% of the agency's 2% |
| Property rental | 25–50% of the agency's 5% |
| International mover | 10–15% of move value |
| Health, life and home insurance | 10–20% of first-year premium |
| Staffing agency | 10–20% of placement fee |
| Interiors and furniture | 10–15% |
| Company formation | 10–20% of setup fee |
| Wealth manager | Introducer fee per a written agreement, **only where its regulator permits it, always disclosed** |
| Doctors, clinics, schools | **No fee.** Never take money for a medical or school recommendation; it poisons trust |

## Rules
- Disclose every referral fee to the client in the engagement letter.
- Recommend the best partner for the family, not the best payer. Keep a note of why each one was chosen.
- Never hold client money, and never take cash.
- Drop any partner after one serious failure. Your reputation is the product.
