## Tax residence notes (not advice; confirm with adviser)
- With no UK family or home, the remaining UK ties are likely the **90-day tie** (more than 90 UK days in either of the previous 2 tax years), plus possibly the **work tie** (40+ UK days with more than 3 hours' work) and the **country tie** (more midnights in the UK than in any other country).
- As a "leaver", the number of ties that makes you UK resident depends on UK days: 1 tie at 121–182 days, 2 at 91–120, 3 at 46–90. With 183+ days you're automatically resident.
- So by cutting UK days to 120 or fewer and keeping UK workdays under 40, you could become non-UK resident from the next tax year (6 Apr 2027), well before the 2–3 year plan.
- Company move: set up the UAE company, invoice through it from the date you become non-resident, then wind down the UK Ltd. Plan how retained profits come out with an adviser.
