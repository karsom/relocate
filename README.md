# Relocate

Private, end-to-end relocation from the UK to Dubai for families and founders worth £5–15m.

One point of contact handles schools, visas, the home, cargo, banking, and life on arrival.

## Offer
| Package | Fee | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Landing | £7,500 | Personalised move plan, visas, school shortlist and applications, rental search, client portal, 30-day landing support |
| Family | £15,000 | Landing + property search (rent or buy), freight/cargo, banking, healthcare, cars, UK exit coordination, 90-day support |
| Founder add-on | +£7,500 | Free-zone company, corporate banking, Golden Visa, introductions to licensed wealth partners |
| Property Care | £500–1,500/mo | Ongoing management of Dubai or UK property through licensed partners |

Partner commissions are disclosed to clients in the engagement letter.
